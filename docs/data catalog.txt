DATA DICTIONARY FOR GOLD LAYER

OVERVIEW:
 The Gold Layer is the business level data representation, structured to support analytical and reporting uses cases. It consists of 
Dimension tables and fact tables for specific business metrics   


1. gold.dim_customers
	Purpose: stores customers details and demographics details
	Columns:
	Column_name	Data Type	Description
	Customer_key	INT	Surrogate key uniquely identifying each customer record in the dimension table
	Customer_id	INT	Unique numerical identifier assigned to each customer
	Customer_rating	DECIMAL	Integers in decimal form rounded upto 1
	region	NVARCHAR(50)	Region of residence where customer resides 
	

2. gold.fact_sales 
	Purpose: Stores transactional sales data for analytical purposes
	Columns:
	Column_name	Data Type	Description
	Customer_key	INT 	Surrogate key linking in order to customer dimension table  
	Customer_id 	INT	Unique numerical identifier assigned to each customer , business key 
	Order_id	INT	Unique key in the table
	Product_category	NVARCHAR(50)	The category product belongs to (e.g: Home, Electronics,etc)
	Order_date	DATE	The Date order got placed 
	quantity	INT	The no .of quantity of the product 
	Unit_price	DECIMAL	Price of single product
	discount	DECIMAL	Discount given on MRP
	Payment_method 	NVARCHAR(50)	The mode of money transaction between customer and business (e.g: COD, wallet)
	Delivery_days 	INT 	The no of business days product got delivered to customers
	Revenue 	DECIMAL	Total money generated per order
